I am currently pursuing a PhD under the supervision of [Alain Trouvé](https://centreborelli.ens-paris-saclay.fr/fr/annuaire-des-personnes/alain-trouve) and [Jean Feydy](https://www.jeanfeydy.com/index.html). I work with both the [Centre Borelli](https://centreborelli.ens-paris-saclay.fr/fr) at the ENS Paris-Saclay and the [HeKa](https://heka.gitlabpages.inria.fr/) Inria team located at Parisanté Campus.  
Previously, I followed a math curriculum at the ENS Ulm and the [MVA](https://www.master-mva.com/) master.

---

## Research interests
- Shape matching and shape interpolation, viewed with the shape space perspective.
- Efficient implementation and accurate discretization of objects from differential or Riemannian geometry, particularly in settings where meshes are not available.

---

## Current focus
- Implementation of elastic-like metrics in the context of point clouds
- Information geometry of elastic metrics
